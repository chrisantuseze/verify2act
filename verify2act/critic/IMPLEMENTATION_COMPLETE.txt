✅ Multi-Headed Architecture
   • Shared encoder (MLP or Transformer)
   • Predicate satisfaction head
   • Action feasibility head  
   • Non-interference head
   • Phased activation (1 → 2 → 3 heads)

✅ Uncertainty Estimation
   • Deep ensemble (5 models default)
   • MC Dropout alternative
   • Epistemic variance: σ² = Var(predictions)
   • Binary entropy: H = -μ log μ - (1-μ) log(1-μ)

✅ Calibrated Reflection Thresholds
   • Hard fail: Low confidence (μ < 0.30-0.40)
   • Uncertainty fail: High epistemic σ or H
   • Soft fail: Borderline μ + moderate σ
   • Per-head customization

✅ Training Pipeline
   • Data collection with hard negatives
   • Multi-head loss with weights
   • Early stopping & checkpointing
   • Calibration (ECE/MCE) tracking

✅ Evaluation Suite
   • Accuracy, precision, recall, F1, AUC
   • Calibration plots & metrics
   • Reflection decision quality
   • Comprehensive reports

✅ System Integration
   • VerifiedPlanner class
   • Multi-iteration reflection loop
   • Candidate sampling & aggregation
   • Targeted prompt generation

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

From 5 Papers (PETS, MBPO, World Models, Dreamer, RWM-U, AV Control):

1. Ensemble Disagreement (RWM-U)
   Use variance of ensemble predictions as epistemic uncertainty

2. Long Rollout Propagation (RWM-U)
   Track uncertainty across full trajectory, penalize high σ²

3. Entropy Gating (AV Paper)
   Binary entropy H is stable control signal for decisions

4. Calibration Critical (Both Papers)
   Threshold tuning essential to avoid over/under-confidence

5. Conservative Planning (PETS, MBPO)
   Short rollouts + uncertainty penalties prevent model bias
